# Dango Derby — guardrails for generated creative

The brand and compliance rules for every Dango Derby ad. Read them before you write
any concept.

## What the game is

A kawaii racing game about Japanese sweets. You steer a glossy dango down twisting
candy courses by swiping. Races last **under a minute**. It is an **iOS app** —
never say "no download", "no install", "it's just a link" or "no app store";
those were true of an earlier web build and are now false. Two modes: **Dango Dash** (wide, sunlit, pastel) and
**Gravity Chase** (vertical falling column, deep navy and gold).

**The one creative idea:** it looks impossibly cute, and the money is real. Almost
every strong spot is that reveal. Don't resolve the tension either way — all-cute is
a kids' toy, all-money is a casino ad.

## Hard compliance — legally material

1. Never use **gambling, casino, bet, wager, jackpot, odds**.
2. Never imply a **player-funded prize pool**. Prizes are fixed and company-funded and
   do not change with entrant count.
3. Never imply **live head-to-head play**. Paid races are asynchronous against
   recordings of real human runs, frozen before entry.
4. Never promise or imply **guaranteed winnings** or frame it as income.
5. Real-money play is **"where eligible"** — region-gated. Don't promise it universally.
6. **Never anything golf.** No putt, hole, tee, flag, golf ball. The game used to be
   minigolf and is not any more. This is the easiest rule to break by accident:
   coastal cliffs, dunes, and manicured parkland all drift golf-shaped with no golf
   word present. Check locations, not just dialogue.
7. Never imply cosmetics affect performance. Skins, faces and trails are purely visual.
8. Use **real footage and screens** from the game. Never fabricate or restyle gameplay.
   No model may generate game UI, and no model may ever draw the wordmark.
9. **Original adult characters only**, in every style, anime too. The game is 18+, so every
   character is clearly an adult. Never use a character or a character design from an anime,
   a manga, a game, a film or any other IP. Never put a character in a school uniform.

## Voice

Two registers, and mixing them is the common mistake.

- **Menu voice** names the fun — Japanese sweets and festival vocabulary, 2–4 words.
- **Dealer voice** handles money — plain verbs, short sentences, no exclamation points
  near a dollar sign.

Rules: CTAs are single verbs (**Play. Claim. Join.**). A sentence about money carries
no joke, no pun, no urgency theatre. **No em dashes** in customer-facing copy. Don't
say "kawaii" — show it. Avoid *crafted, elevate, curated, timeless*.

## Safe payoff pattern

Small real amounts ($2.40, $1.80) matching the actual payout screen, and a
self-deprecating line about the size of the win. This is funnier *and* it is the
compliant framing — it cannot read as an income claim.

## Angles that fit

Bait-and-switch reveal · races last under a minute · clutch Gravity Chase near-miss ·
collection flex · the league ladder's seven painted worlds · scale wonder (tiny racer,
giant world) · "one more run" · free to start.

**Avoid:** anything that reads as a casino ad, anything implying guaranteed money,
fabricated gameplay, any golf reference.
